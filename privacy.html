Privacy policy

This is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, to upload videos and read the channel's statistics.

It does not collect, store, share or sell data from any other person. Access credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private repository and are not shared with third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
